About

I am interested in developing reliable machine learning methods for complex biomedical data.

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice University, advised by Prof. Wei Qiu. My research interests lie in medical artificial intelligence, with a focus on foundation models and multimodal learning for biomedical applications.
